Problem

Buyers pay for capacity they can't see is about to fail

Hidden obsolete parts

Machines run on controllers, drives and screens the manufacturer stopped making, and no one has a spare.

Surprise CapEx after close

Sellers freeze equipment spending 12–18 months before a sale. A buyer found a +105% CapEx adjustment before close.

Weeks-long outages

PLC lead times of 10–24 weeks. 44% of plants have equipment interruptions every month.
