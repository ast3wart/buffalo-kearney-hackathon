*November 14, 2026*

## Per-site price benchmarks

**Default basis:** one mid-sized manufacturing site, **10 paid CMMS users**, and a 12-month budget. CMMS figures below are license-only; a one-off assessment or consulting project is shown as **year-one spend**, not as a recurring subscription. Prices are USD unless marked otherwise. These products and services do different jobs, so the figures should not be treated as like-for-like bids.

## MRO inventory-optimization consulting

A published **consultancy market guide**, rather than a vendor quote, gives these one-off engagement ranges. For a single site, budget **$35,000–$90,000 in year one** for a spend/opportunity diagnostic, **$60,000–$150,000** for storeroom and inventory optimization alone, or **$80,000–$220,000** for a broader single-site MRO program. The guide also reports gain-share arrangements of **15%–30% of validated savings**. These are **project costs incurred within a year**, not fees that necessarily repeat annually; a three-year accounting allocation of the $60,000–$150,000 optimization project would be $20,000–$50,000 per site-year, but would not change the cash paid for the project. [21](https://apsentra.com/mro-procurement-consulting/)

**Bottom line:** Public prices support a fairly tight **CMMS license benchmark**, but **not a numeric SPARETECH or Verusen price**. For OEM work, keep a **one-off installed-base evaluation**, a **digital lifecycle subscription**, and a **support agreement** in separate budget lines; their published and reported figures represent materially different scopes.
