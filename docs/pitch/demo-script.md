# Demo script (about 4 min)

| Time | Who | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | B | **Hook:** "This company thinks the UV-400 costs $X to build. Over its life it costs $Y. And one supplier is about to make that a lot worse." |
| 0:30 | A | **The mess:** show 3 raw rows: a BOM line, a spare-part PO, a claim with no part number. "Three teams, three systems, no links." |
| 1:00 | A | **Link it:** run the matching. "We linked N% of service spend and N% of claims, at P% precision, measured against planted correct answers." |
| 1:45 | B | **The alert:** "Seal kit SK-220: claims up 3× across 7 products since March builds. No single product team could see this." |
| 2:30 | B | **The brief:** generate it. Warranty recovery of $X plus spare-part markup of $Y. "This is what you take into Monday's supplier meeting." |
| 3:15 | A | **Real-world grounding:** "217 US public companies reported warranty payments in 2024. Ford alone paid $5.8B." Velocity research slide. |
| 3:45 | B | **Close:** "Every BOM team shows you what a product costs. We show you the problem coming, and who pays for it." |
