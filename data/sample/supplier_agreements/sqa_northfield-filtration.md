# Supplier Quality Agreement
Prairie Utility Vehicles ("Buyer") and Northfield Filtration, supplier no. S-031 ("Supplier"), US. Effective 2025-01-01.

## 4. Quality
4.1 Supplier maintains IATF 16949 certification and ships with a certificate of conformance per lot.

## 7. Warranty
7.1 Supplier warrants each part for 12 months from the date of manufacture of the end unit in which it is installed.
7.2 For field failures attributable to Supplier's parts, Supplier reimburses 50% of the parts only; dealer labor is excluded.
7.3 Recovery claims are submitted within 30 days after Buyer pays the dealer claim.
7.4 Freight, handling and administrative fees are not recoverable.
