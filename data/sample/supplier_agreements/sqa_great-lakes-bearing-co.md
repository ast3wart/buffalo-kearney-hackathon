# Supplier Quality Agreement
Prairie Utility Vehicles ("Buyer") and Great Lakes Bearing Co, supplier no. S-011 ("Supplier"), US. Effective 2025-01-01.

## 4. Quality
4.1 Supplier maintains IATF 16949 certification and ships with a certificate of conformance per lot.

## 7. Warranty
7.1 Supplier warrants each part for 24 months from the date of manufacture of the end unit in which it is installed.
7.2 For field failures attributable to Supplier's parts, Supplier reimburses 100% of parts and dealer labor, labor at the hours actually claimed and capped at USD 95 per hour.
7.3 Recovery claims are submitted within 60 days after Buyer pays the dealer claim.
7.4 Freight, handling and administrative fees are not recoverable.
